## 🏛️ The 6 Core Pedagogical Pillars

### 1. High-Yield Visual Cram Synthesis (Zero-Fluff Mnemonic Architecture)
* **The Anti-Pattern:** 800-page dense textbook PDFs that bury high-impact exam concepts in verbose narrative explanations, leading to cognitive fatigue and passive reading illusions.
* **The Cram Exam Standard:** Every technical concept must be condensed into a single-glance memory matrix, keyword triggers, or visual cheat sheet.
* **Requirements:**
  * **"If You See X, Think Y" Anchors:** Rapid associative triggers for exam scenarios (e.g., *"If you see centralized AAA with dial-in / VPN, think RADIUS (802.1X); if you see TACACS+, think Cisco port 49 TCP and separated AuthN/AuthZ"*).
  * **High-Density Comparison Tables:** Group confusable technologies side-by-side (e.g., symmetric vs asymmetric ciphers, RAID levels, AWS S3 storage tiers, OpenShift routes vs ingresses).
  * **Port & Protocol Speed Cards:** Core network services pinned with port number, transport layer protocol (TCP/UDP), and cryptographic standard.

---

### 2. Domain-Weighted Objective Mastery
* **The Anti-Pattern:** Treating all exam topics equally, spending days on obscure trivia that only accounts for 4% of an exam while neglecting core 30% domains.
* **The Cram Exam Standard:** Every curriculum must map directly to the official vendor examination blueprint with explicit percentage weightings.
* **Blueprint Standards Across Projects:**
  1. **CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701):**
     * Domain 1: General Security Concepts (12%)
     * Domain 2: Threats, Vulnerabilities & Mitigations (22%)
     * Domain 3: Security Architecture (18%)

  2. **Red Hat Certified System Administrator (RHCSA EX200):**
     * Competency 1: Essential Tools & Core Commands (15%)
     * Competency 2: Operating Running Systems & Systemd (20%)
     * Competency 3: Storage Configuration, LVM & VDO (20%)
     * Competency 4: File Systems & Mounts (15%)
     * Competency 5: User & Group Security Hardening (10%)
     * Competency 6: Firewalld, SELinux & SSH (15%)
     * Competency 7: Basic Container Management with Podman (5%)
  3. **AWS Certified Solutions Architect & Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02 / SAA):**
     * Domain 1: Cloud Concepts (24%)
     * Domain 2: Security & Compliance (30%)
     * Domain 3: Cloud Technology & Services (34%)

  4. **Red Hat OpenShift & Enterprise Containers (DO180 / DO280 / DO380):**

### 3. Immediate-Feedback Active Recall Loops
* **The Anti-Pattern:** Taking a 100-question test and only seeing the raw score at the very end with no explanation of why missed answers were wrong.
* **The Cram Exam Standard:** The fastest path to long-term memory consolidation is immediate corrective feedback at the moment of recall.
* **Requirements:**
  * **Instant Question Rationale:** When a user selects an answer option, immediately reveal:
    1. **Why the Correct Answer is Right:** Grounded in vendor documentation and RFC standards.
    2. **Why Distractors are Incorrect:** Explicitly highlighting the subtle tricks, mismatched protocols, or obsolete configurations.
  * **Passage Highlighting:** Underline the key scenario constraints (e.g., *"least operational overhead"*, *"stateful vs stateless"*, *"non-repudiation"*).

---

### 4. Performance-Based Scenario Drills (PBQs & CLI)
* **The Anti-Pattern:** Pure rote memorization of trivia that collapses the moment a candidate faces a practical terminal or drag-and-drop scenario.
* **The Cram Exam Standard:** Hands-on command execution, declarative manifest authoring, and simulated interactive configuration.
* **Requirements:**
  * **CompTIA PBQs:** Firewall rule ordering, network topology cabling, SIEM log triage, and certificate chain validation.
  * **Red Hat Terminal Quests:** Hands-on mock exams executed in real bash terminals (`set -euo pipefail`, `nmcli`, `firewall-cmd --permanent`, `tuned-adm profile`, `semanage fcontext`).
  * **OpenShift Manifest Challenges:** Writing declarative YAML for Deployments, PersistentVolumeClaims, and SecurityContextConstraints.

### 6. Spatial Traversal & The Method of Loci (Memory Palace)
* **Cognitive Science Foundation:** Spatial cognition is among human memory's most potent evolutionarily conserved faculties. By anchoring distinct knowledge disciplines (CompTIA, Red Hat, AWS, Scripting, AI, Reverse Engineering) to physical framed exhibits in a designated corridor, spatial recall reinforces conceptual boundary formation and eliminates cross-discipline interference.
* **The 3-Phase Choreographed Human Walk:**
  1. **Phase 1 (Aisle Re-centering):** The camera smoothly slides backwards from the painting stance onto the central carpet runner and turns its focal angle down the museum corridor.
  2. **Phase 2 (Biomechanical Stride):** The learner strides forward down the hall with realistic vertical head-bob (0.04m amplitude) and lateral sway, accompanied by synchronized 1K hardwood footstep foley (`assets/footsteps/footstep_01.ogg` through `08.ogg`).
  3. **Phase 3 (Station Arrival & Focus):** Upon reaching the target exhibit's coordinates, the camera pivots gracefully toward the wall, squares up to the painting, and smoothly reveals the station telemetry HUD.
